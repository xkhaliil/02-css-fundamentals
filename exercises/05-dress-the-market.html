3 · A plan that works

  1. Variables & fonts. Write :root with your palette. Add a Google Fonts <link> in the <head>, before your stylesheet — e.g. <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bricolage+Grotesque&display=swap">. Set font-family and color on body — inheritance does the rest.
  2. Type. Sizes for h1, h2, h3, .lead, .eyebrow. Line-heights. Letter-spacing on small caps labels.
  3. Boxes. Padding and borders on .vendor-card, the table, the form. max-width + margin: 0 auto on main (shorthand: top/bottom 0, left/right auto = centred).
  4. States. :hover, :focus-visible, details[open], input:user-invalid.
  5. Details. Pseudo-elements, :nth-child rhythms, :has() tricks.
