Colour

Colour with three knobs

HSL = hue (where on the wheel), saturation (how colourful), lightness (how bright). Change one knob at a time. The contrast numbers tell you whether text on this colour is readable.

Check your pair

Your text colour on your background — the check Exercise 5a asks for. Paste hex values from your :root.

WCAG asks for at least 4.5 : 1 contrast for body text and 3 : 1 for large headings. Click a square in the palette to jump to that lightness.
