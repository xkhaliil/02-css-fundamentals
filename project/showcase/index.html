Harbour Night Market

Forty stalls, three stages and one very long table on the waterfront at Moll de la Fusta. Street food, vinyl, handmade things and people you haven't met yet.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Entry is free. Bring cash or a card for the stalls.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, on a leash. The dumplings are not for dogs.

What if it rains?

We move under the big tent next to the main stage. The market has never closed early.
